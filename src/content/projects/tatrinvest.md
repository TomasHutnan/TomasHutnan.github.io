Tatrinvest was built at Hack Košice 2024 as an investment app for beginners. Users can buy and sell stocks in a simple interface. An LLM assistant answers questions and suggests investment strategies based on user preferences. Stock recommendations came from polynomials fitted to market data to estimate short-term trends. Goals, rewards and challenges make investing more engaging, and financial literacy features help users learn the basics.
